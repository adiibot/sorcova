Before the feeling has a name

“I’m not stressed.
Just a bit tired.”

Their biology tells another story.

What feels like ordinary tiredness can sit beside a disrupted daily rhythm. The point is not to diagnose a feeling from one curve. It is to notice that the biological context may deserve a closer, clinician-led look.

Biology is time

The same marker can mean something different at a different hour.

Explore two simplified rhythms. The shape, timing and relationship between measurements can carry information that a single isolated value cannot.

Intact patternSharp rise. Predictable decline.

A clear awakening response is followed by a gradual reduction toward night.

Disturbed patternBlunted rise. Rhythm stays elevated.

The daily signal is flatter, with less morning separation and a later lift.

Systems keep time together
