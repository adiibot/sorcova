Why Sorcova

The biology moves before the feeling has a name.

“I’m not stressed. Just a bit tired.”

Sometimes that is all a person can name while their biology may already be changing. Sorcova is designed to make those changes more legible—without reducing a complex human story to a single score.

A single reading is useful, but incomplete. By reading biomarkers in context—across rhythms, confidence, confounds and, over time, repeated observations—we aim to move from a snapshot toward a personal baseline.

A standard panel shows what it measures on the day. Sorcova looks at the rhythms and relationships that can begin to shift earlier—before the change is easy to feel or name.

The architectural principle

AI speaks last.
Safety gates the microphone.

Deterministic engines produce structured analytical outputs upstream. As Sorcova develops toward Class IIa, the architecture is designed so clinical interpretation and decisions remain with the responsible clinician, while generative AI stays behind a can-block safety layer.
